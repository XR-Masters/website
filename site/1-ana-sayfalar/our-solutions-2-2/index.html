Location-aware AI Agents

We bring AI into the physical world with location-aware agents that understand where users are and what is around them. They can guide, inform, recommend, and interact naturally with people across museums, retail spaces, airports, cruise ports, stadiums, cultural heritage sites, and other real-world environments.

Our Location-Aware AI Agents combine conversational AI with precise spatial awareness, allowing them to understand not only what a user is asking, but also where the user is and what is around them.

Powered by MyGeoVerse, AI agents can discover Points of Interest and access location-specific information defined by the organization. This allows an agent to recognize that a visitor is approaching a particular exhibit, store, gate, landmark, product, or other point of interest and provide relevant information, answer questions, make recommendations, or guide the user to the next destination.

Experiences can be audio-first for natural, hands-free interaction or include a 3D AI Avatar when a visual guide is preferred. The same spatial foundation can support smartphones, XR headsets, AI glasses, and emerging spatial interfaces.

Using technologies including Visual Positioning Systems (VPS), GeoPose, spatial anchors, image markers, and QR codes, MyGeoVerse can provide precise spatial context with accuracy of up to 10 cm in supported environments.

From AI tour guides and venue assistants to retail agents, navigation assistants, and interactive brand characters, Location-Aware AI Agents turn AI from something users access on a screen into something that can understand and interact with the physical world around them.

AR Way Finding

Go beyond the blue dot. AR Wayfinding provides intuitive, real-world navigation using visual or audio guidance, helping people find exactly where they need to go across complex indoor and outdoor environments.

Traditional maps can tell users approximately where they are. MyGeoVerse AR Wayfinding can guide them through the physical environment with precise spatial awareness.

Instead of constantly looking down at a 2D map, users can follow visual AR directions placed directly into their surroundings or choose audio-first guidance for a more natural, hands-free experience.

Using GPS for broader outdoor positioning and technologies such as Visual Positioning Systems (VPS), GeoPose, spatial anchors, image markers, and QR codes where greater precision is required, MyGeoVerse can support positioning accuracy of up to 10 cm in enabled environments.

Routes can connect entrances, gates, stores, exhibits, offices, meeting rooms, transportation points, or any other defined Point of Interest, making the solution suitable for airports, shopping centers, campuses, stadiums, museums, tourism destinations, and large enterprise facilities.

Built on the MyGeoVerse geospatial platform, the same navigation layer can extend across smartphones, XR headsets, AI glasses, and emerging spatial interfaces.

AR Live Presentations

One presenter. One shared immersive experience. Lead your audience through synchronized AR presentations, transition seamlessly into VR, and return to the real world without ever losing control of the presentation.

Immersive presentations are powerful, but there is a problem. Put a headset on someone, especially for the first time, and the technology itself can quickly become the center of attention. Instead of listening to the presenter, participants start exploring the environment, experimenting with controllers, and following their own path.

AR Live Presentations is designed differently.

One presenter controls the experience for the entire audience. Participants see the same synchronized 3D content while the presenter controls what appears, when it appears, and where the presentation goes next.

Start with a shared AR presentation in the physical room. When the story calls for something more immersive, the presenter can take the entire audience into a VR environment to experience a product, property, destination, design, or scenario. When that part of the story is complete, everyone can return to AR and the presenter simply continues the presentation.

Participants do not need controllers or training. They can look around naturally and experience the content, but the presentation remains presenter-led. This keeps attention on the story, the product, and the person presenting rather than on learning how to operate the technology.

For sales presentations in particular, this changes the role of XR. Instead of competing for the audience’s attention, immersive technology becomes part of the presenter’s storytelling.

AR Live Presentations can be used for product demonstrations, sales meetings, architectural and real estate presentations, destination showcases, executive briefings, training, and other situations where showing and experiencing something together can communicate more than slides alone.
AR Live Presentations supports Meta Quest 3 and is being expanded to support Meta VR Glass beginning in Spring 2027, extending the same presenter-controlled experience to the next generation of immersive hardware.

Events

Turn physical events into interactive spatial experiences. From location-aware AI agents and AR wayfinding to shared immersive content, XR Masters brings a digital layer directly into the event environment.

Events bring large numbers of people together in one physical environment, making them ideal spaces for location-aware and shared immersive experiences.

XR Masters can create a spatial digital layer across conferences, exhibitions, trade shows, festivals, product launches, corporate events, and other live environments. Visitors can discover interactive content based on where they are, receive guidance through the venue, interact with AI agents, explore products and exhibits in AR, and participate in shared experiences with other attendees.

Location-Aware AI Agents can act as event assistants, helping visitors discover exhibitors, sessions, products, services, and points of interest while answering questions through natural conversation.

AR Wayfinding can guide attendees through complex venues using visual or audio directions, while persistent AR content can transform booths, stages, meeting areas, and public spaces into interactive digital touchpoints.

For product launches, demonstrations, and VIP presentations, AR Live Presentations can also be integrated into the event experience, allowing presenters to lead groups through synchronized AR and VR content.

Because these capabilities can operate on the same MyGeoVerse spatial foundation, an event does not have to become a collection of disconnected XR experiences. AI agents, navigation, spatial content, and shared experiences can all reference the same physical environment and Points of Interest.

The result is an event experience where the digital layer becomes part of the venue itself, helping visitors navigate, discover, interact, and engage with the people, brands, and content around them.
